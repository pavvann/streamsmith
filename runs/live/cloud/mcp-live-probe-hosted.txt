sink is still catching up from its start block, so the committed MCP continues to serve the

What this probe does not show
-----------------------------

tool answered under the shipped maxLagBlocks of 300. That check, and the cut-over of
packages/mcp-vaultflows and the Vaultpilot dashboard to `vaultflows_hosted`, are the remaining steps
once lag falls below 300.
